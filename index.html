Investigation

How to explore (optional)
  1. Explore the strata: Inspect layers and compare fossil samples.
  2. Look for a change: Notice where abundance or diversity drops.
  3. Mark the boundary: Choose where a mass extinction likely occurred.
  4. Run a simulation: Change how quickly and severely conditions shift.
  5. Compare outcomes: See survival, biodiversity, and newly formed layers.

This is a simplified fictional model. Fossil samples are clues, and results are not predictions for real organisms.
